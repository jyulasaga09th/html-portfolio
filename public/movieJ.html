The Best Movies According to Josephine

My Top 3 Movies of All Time

Pretty Woman

It is the movie that made Julia Roberts famous. It tells the story of a sex worker and a businessman who find love despite their differences in social status.

Howl's Moving Castle

It is one of the best anime films about a girl who is cursed to become an old woman and a sorcerer who takes her into his moving house. As they learn to discover and accept who they are, they also find themselves falling for each other.

Edge of Tomorrow (Live Die Repeat)

It is a science fiction movie starring Tom Cruise. It shows how he finds the courage to fight an alien invasion, but he must relive the same day over and over again until he and his team are able to win the war.
